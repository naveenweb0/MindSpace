Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 2026

1. What we collect

When you book a session or buy a membership, we collect the details you give us — your name, mobile number and (optionally) your email address, plus your booking details (activity, date, time, number of guests and payment amount).

2. How we use it

We use this information only to manage your booking, confirm your session, and contact you about your visit. We may send booking confirmations by email or WhatsApp where you've provided those details.

3. Payments

Payments are processed through our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not store your full card, UPI or net-banking credentials on our servers.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share only what is necessary with service providers who help us run bookings and payments, and we never share your details for marketing purposes.

5. Retention

We keep booking records as needed to operate the studio and meet legal requirements. You can request deletion of your personal details at any time by contacting us.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.

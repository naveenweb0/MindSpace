Our space

A space built for slowing down.

Warm light, a table you're allowed to make a mess at, and everything you need within reach.

The main table

A table waiting to be messy.

Big, sturdy and happily stained from a hundred little projects. There's always a spot, always a fresh canvas, and nobody watching your brushwork.

The quiet corner

A corner for quiet.

An armchair, floor cushions and low light. When your hands need a break, or you just want to sit with a cup of something warm and breathe.

The sound

A little music in the background.

Soft, unhurried music floats through the studio. Or plug into our listening corner with good headphones and let the rest of the world disappear for a while.

The materials

Materials ready to be played with.

Paints, clay, paper, yarn, tools — all out in the open and all included. Take what you need, put it back when you're done. Simple.

Pictures are nice. The real thing is better.
